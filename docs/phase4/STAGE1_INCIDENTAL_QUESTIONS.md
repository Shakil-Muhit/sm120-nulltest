# Stage-1 incidental observations and unresolved questions

Recorded during the Phase-4 execution (2026-09-22) as the owner's two-stage instruction asks; they are inputs to Stage 2, not results. Numbers refer to `docs/phase4/EXPERIMENT_REPORT_session4.md` and its evidence paths.

1. **Matched-runner context changes what is measured for graph-breaking arms.** In one process, the second-built C1/C1P arm re-enters the first arm's dynamo frames, exhausts `recompile_limit` (8) on the model's top-level `forward` frame (mel_band_roformer.py:127) and runs that frame eager: +215 kernels/pass, 51 vs 96 graph launches, +14.6 ms GPU-busy at 8 s. Evidence: session-3 `torchlogs_matched_C1_C1P_352800_m1.log` ([7/8] recompile-limit warning in the second build only), session-4 PB records (both build orders), `matched_trace_C1_C1P_352800_p1.json`. Question: can a bounded diagnostic (per-frame recompile counters + kernel-count/graph-launch deltas between standalone and in-context builds) certify a ranking as context-free? Not verified by intervention (raising the limit).
2. **Freezing gain is not one-for-one with removed parameter casts.** At 8 s, freezing+no-inlining is worth 1.30 (reduce-overhead), 1.31 (max-autotune) and 1.36 ms (pad-and-batch) — the same size on three bases — while the traced cast time in C3-family arms is 0.915 ms/pass; at 100 ms the gain is 0.74 (C3I→C3G) vs 1.03/1.04 on the other bases, with 0.85 ms of casts. Question: what does freezing remove beyond the casts (rotary cos/sin folding, norm+cast fusion, launch count 1143→729 were seen in Phase 3), and why is the 100 ms gain base-dependent? Trace class deltas from session 4 (C3I vs C3G, H vs HG) to be examined.
3. **Inlining off alone is a small, consistent cost** (−0.2 ms at 8 s, −0.09 ms at 100 ms; below thresholds). Question: which frames/guards change when `inline_inbuilt_nn_modules=False` (more graph-level modules, different fusion boundaries)?
4. **Ranking flip between 6 s and 8 s on the same GPU** (H faster by 0.45 at 6 s; C3 faster by 0.33 at 8 s), with a FAILED affine predictor (E9). Question: is the crossover driven by the pad-and-batch overhead growing with frames (padding waste) versus the per-band launch overhead saturating — a property of band count × frames rather than of this checkpoint? Only a boundary test on a different band layout / frame count could tell.
5. **max-autotune on sm120 rejects triton_mm configs for shared memory** ("Required: 131072/147456/110592, Hardware limit: 101376") — the consumer Blackwell (sm_120) shared-memory budget is smaller than data-center parts; the autotuner's candidate set is partly wasted and the chosen configs may be suboptimal. Question: does the sm120 shared-memory limit systematically change which GEMM configs win (vs sm_100/sm_90), and would a sm120-aware config space change the C3M/C3MG result? Observed in the smoke and session logs (`torchlogs_C3MG_*`).
6. **Driver difference across pods** (580.159.03 vs 580.178.04) with unchanged torch/triton: any driver effect is confounded with the physical GPU; A/A controls look alike across sessions (−0.009 vs −0.09/−0.06 ms). No action; provenance only.
7. **Profiler perturbation at 8 s is tiny** (0.02–0.24 ms on 37–53 ms blocks) in the profiled matched process; at 100 ms it was larger in Phase 3 (traced C3I 4.10 vs 3.62 ms unprofiled in the smoke, caches disabled). Question: how much of the 100 ms perturbation is cache-disabled recompilation artefacts vs nsys overhead? Relevant for any 100 ms attribution.
8. **Autotune configs are chosen at compile time from one shape**; the C3 family compiles a fresh graph per frame count. Question (deployment): does the autotune choice at one frame count transfer to neighbouring frame counts? Not measured.

9. **Freezing slows the GEMM class** (C3I → C3G: −0.13 ms at 8 s, −0.37 ms at 100 ms in device time) while removing casts and launches. Question: with constant (frozen) weights, does Inductor/cuBLAS pick different GEMM kernels or layouts (e.g., the weight cast now folded into a constant with a different stride/alignment), and is the effect shape-dependent (largest at the small 100 ms shapes)? `docs/phase4/inventory_diff_session4.json`, signatures_changed rows; not tested by intervention.
10. **Freezing on the max-autotune base moves GEMMs from cuBLAS extern calls into Triton templates** (session-3 C3M: 178 templates + 145 extern at 8 s; session-4 C3MG: 285 + 15) — cross-session, descriptive. Question: is the template/extern split decided by the autotuner's benchmark under constant weights (different candidate set), and does the split, rather than the cast removal, explain why C3MG is the fastest configuration (23.56 ms at 8 s)?
11. **Verdicts near the declared threshold with three restarts** (PA 8 s: point −0.331 vs 0.30 with restart medians 0.297–0.332; PC1 8 s: −0.206, unresolved): the registered rule is met or missed by a few hundredths of a millisecond, and with n = 3 restarts the cluster-bootstrap interval is the restart-median spread. Question (methodological): what restart count makes a threshold verdict stable, and should thresholds be declared relative to the A/A spread rather than in absolute ms?
